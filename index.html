About

Who's behind this.

I'm a third-year MEng Chemical Engineering student at the University of Nottingham, currently First Class, spending this year on academic exchange at the China campus in Ningbo.

My focus is process design and data-driven systems, with a growing interest in how that applies to more sustainable engineering. I like using data to make decisions clearer, not just faster, and the same instinct carries into how I lead. As VP of the Women's Engineering Society and Sponsorship Liaison for Frank Morton 2026, I care as much about how something is communicated as how it's built.

Outside of engineering, I paint and draw, most of it stays in sketchbooks rather than anywhere public, and take on graphic design work like this site, which I designed and built myself. I like work that sits at the intersection of structure and creativity, whether that's a process diagram or a page layout.
